Common Questions

Clear answers before we start

Transparency is our first principle. Here is how we price, plan, and execute projects.

How do you structure project pricing?
Every build is scoped explicitly. Clearly defined projects receive a fixed milestone-based estimate. Evolving or ongoing products operate on flexible weekly sprint rates. The initial technical consultation is free, and we never start without a written specification.
What does a typical delivery timeline look like?
Production MVPs typically ship in four to eight weeks depending on technical depth. Rapid working prototypes — like our hackathon builds — can be demoed in days. We provide realistic, data-backed estimates rather than overly optimistic projections.
Can you plug into our existing codebase or engineers?
Yes. We frequently act as an autonomous delivery squad or augment existing teams in specialized domains like AI pipeline design, backend optimization, or design systems. We conduct a preliminary architectural review first to ensure alignment.
We are outside Ogbomosho / international — how do we collaborate?
We work remote-first with asynchronous updates, shared project tracking boards, and weekly live demo sessions. Our delivery model ensures you always have complete visibility regardless of time zones.
What happens after our product is launched?
You receive complete source code, deployment scripts, and handover documentation. If ongoing support is needed, we offer dedicated maintenance retainers covering infrastructure monitoring, performance tuning, and feature iterations.
