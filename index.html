Questions

Before we start

The things people usually ask us first.

How do you price projects?

Every project is scoped individually. Well-defined builds get a fixed price; open-ended or evolving work runs on a weekly rate. The first consultation is free, and you'll always get a written estimate before anything starts.

How long does a typical build take?

Most MVPs take four to eight weeks depending on scope. A working prototype — the kind we build at hackathons — can come together in days. We'll give you a realistic timeline in the first call rather than an optimistic one.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Yes. We can plug in as a full squad, or fill specific gaps — AI, backend, frontend, or design. If you already have a codebase, we'll review it first and tell you honestly whether we can add value.

We're not in Ogbomosho — can we still work together?

Absolutely. We work remote-first with async updates, a shared board, and a weekly demo so you can see progress without waiting for a meeting. Time zones haven't been a problem so far.

What happens after launch?

You get handover documentation either way. If you want us to stay on, we offer ongoing maintenance and iteration — monitoring, fixes, and new features as your product grows.

Do you take on small projects?

Yes. We're five people, so we deliberately keep a small number of projects at a time — which means even a single-feature build gets real attention. Tell us what you need and we'll say plainly if it's a fit.
